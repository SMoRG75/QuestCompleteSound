[size=5][b]QuestCompleteSound[/b][/size]

[b]Hear your quest progress:[/b] a worker voice line when you complete a quest objective, and another when the whole quest is done. That's all it does.

Works in Retail and WoW Forever.


[size=4][b]Moved to SMoRG's QoL[/b][/size]

All the other settings have moved to my addon [url=https://www.wowinterface.com/downloads/info27251]SMoRG's QoL[/url]:
[list]
[*]Quest progress colors
[*]Automatic quest tracking
[*]Hide completed achievements
[*]Splash screen
[*]Debug output
[*]Chat messages when a quest is done
[/list]

SMoRG's QoL plays the same sounds, so you only need one of the addons. If both are loaded, QuestCompleteSound stays silent so you don't hear every sound twice.


[size=4][b]Sounds[/b][/size]

[b]Horde (Peon)[/b], the default
[list]
[*]Objective complete: [i]"Work, work."[/i]
[*]Quest complete: [i]"Work complete."[/i]
[/list]

[b]Alliance (Human worker)[/b]
[list]
[*]Objective complete: [i]"More work?"[/i]
[*]Quest complete: [i]"Job's done!"[/i]
[/list]

When the last objective of a quest is completed, you only hear the quest complete sound.


[size=4][b]Options window[/b][/size]

Turn the sounds on or off and choose the sound profile in the options window. Open it from:
[list]
[*]the addon compartment menu by the minimap
[*][b]Esc -> Options -> AddOns -> QuestCompleteSound[/b]
[*][b]/qcs config[/b]
[/list]


[size=4][b]Chat commands[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]/qcs questsound[/b] or [b]/qcs qs[/b] - toggle the quest completion sound
[*][b]/qcs objectivesound[/b] or [b]/qcs os[/b] - toggle the objective completion sound
[*][b]/qcs soundprofile[/b] - switch between the Horde and Alliance sounds
[*][b]/qcs config[/b] - open the options window
[*][b]/qcs reset[/b] - reset all settings to defaults
[*][b]/qcs help[/b] - display the command overview
[/list]
